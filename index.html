About the School

Building Champions
Since 2012

Shan Badminton School has been Sri Lanka's premier junior badminton academy for over a decade, blending technical mastery with mental fortitude to produce athletes ready for national and international competition.


Our mission is simple: elevate every player through elite coaching, modern training methods, and a relentless commitment to excellence — from their first swing to the competitive stage.

Programs for ages 5+, beginner to advanced
Strength & conditioning tailored to badminton
Personalised skill development & competition plans
National & international tournament preparation
Small groups for focused individual attention
Head Coach — Shan SLBA Certified · National & International

With extensive national-level playing experience and a proven track record, Shan leads every program with expertise and precision. He specialises in advanced technique, tactical awareness, and performance conditioning — tailored to each player's goals.

Assistant Coaches Trained Professionals · Competitive Experience

Our Assistant Coaches are trained professionals who bring strong competitive experience and coaching knowledge to the court. Skilled in advanced technique development, strength and conditioning, and tactical training, they ensure that every athlete receives structured, goal-focused support.
